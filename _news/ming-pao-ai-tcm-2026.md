On **16 November 2026**, **Ming Pao** featured an interview with **Prof. Lu Zhang** and **Wei Liu**, co-founder and CEO of **BioMap**, on **AI for Traditional Chinese Medicine (TCM)** research.

The report discusses the planned **HKBU–BioMap AI for TCM joint laboratory**, highlighting two research directions:

- Investigating bioactive components and their links to therapeutic targets in animal-derived TCM medicines, starting with spiders.
- Modelling intestinal cells' responses to TCM formulations to help prioritise candidates for further experimental study.
