Prof. Lu Zhang participated as a panelist in **Hong Kong's Bio-computing Year in Review** at BioMap's **International Bio-Computing Innovation Summit 2026**, held on **6 November 2026** at the Hong Kong Convention and Exhibition Centre.

He also joined BioMap co-founder and CEO **Wei Liu** for interviews with the **South China Morning Post** and **Ming Pao**, discussing **AI for Genomics** and **AI for Traditional Chinese Medicine (TCM)**.
