My research sits at the intersection of AI and the life sciences, developing deep
learning and generative models for metagenomics, multi-modal large language models,
diffusion probabilistic models and self-supervised representation learning.

## Selected work

- **TaCoGPT** - a LLaMA-based generative model for taxonomic annotation of MAG contigs (ongoing)
